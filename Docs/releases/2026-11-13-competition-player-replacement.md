# Admin competition player replacement

Open Competitions → competition → Entries → Replace player. Choose the outgoing player, pick a replacement, supply their individual handicap when required, and give a reason. Review and confirm. The existing entry ID, draw references, draw order and payment status remain. Names and calculated handicap totals refresh on all draw uses. The picker excludes existing entrants and the RPC independently checks duplicates and active non-guest membership.

Admin/superuser authorization is enforced by the server. Stale name/member/handicap snapshots are rejected. Any recorded/submitted/completed result prevents replacement. An existing scorecard setup/shared card, a started/cancelled/archived linked fixture, or an inconsistent fixture player list requires separate resolution. No historical result is silently reattributed.

Eligible future linked fixtures retain their date, time and rink. The replacement inherits the outgoing playing position, starts with a pending RSVP, and replaces any outgoing Captain/Vice assignment. A non-playing Admin organiser remains unchanged. Standard booking confirmations are queued to the corrected participants. Other unlinked fixtures are unaffected.

Published corrections queue amendment notices to the corrected entry and affected opponents, with refreshed contacts/handicap information. The removed player receives a removal notice without opponents' contact information. Draft corrections do not queue emails. Unsent frozen competition messages and stale fixture messages/actions are cancelled. In-flight claimed competition mail blocks replacement until it finishes. Existing rate limiting, worker authentication and staging's seven-address allowlist remain in force.

An append-only Admin-readable audit records before/after entry members, reason, actor, time and affected fixture IDs. The RPC is authenticated-only, with no direct client insert/update grant on the audit table. Recovery can disable new calls using scripts/recovery_competition_player_replacement.sql while retaining evidence and cancelled statuses.

Staging first: rollback-only synthetic integration covers draft/published corrections, booked handicap fixtures, payment/order preservation, duplicate/stale/non-Admin/played/scoring guards, in-flight mail protection, RSVP updates, stale-mail cancellation and privileges. App tests cover review, cancellation, partner order, handicap totals, picker exclusions and Admin-only controls. Email tests cover both entries, removal notices, production recipients and staging restrictions. No real competition has been amended as part of implementation.
