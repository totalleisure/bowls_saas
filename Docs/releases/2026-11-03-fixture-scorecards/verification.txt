Fixture-linked scorecards: first integration, 3 November 2026

Scope
Yellow Score Card button in the Member Internal Fixture header, restricted to internal, member-bookable, preselect rink fixtures. Selected players/opponents and Club Admins may prepare a card for their rink. Singles prefill both names; pairs prefill both partnerships with the recorded skips as suggested signature names. Signatures still require actual user input. Initial creation needs a connection. Local scoring and cold-start recovery work offline afterwards.

A private match parent and per-rink setup provide a multi-rink structure. The first scorer reviews the conditions; later scorers reuse the immutable setup with their own independent local scores. Booking duration is not inferred as playing time. Fractional handicaps require further support and are rejected instead of rounded.

This revision does not upload live scores, compare scorers, provide spectator viewing, or submit/confirm official fixture or competition results. It sends no messages. Existing scoring continues independently.

New private tables have RLS and no direct client privileges; authenticated RPCs enforce membership and per-rink scoring permission. Cascades avoid blocking original fixture/rink deletion; deleting an auth user clears setup creator attribution.
