Total Leisure Bowls — What's New in 1.6.7 (33)
Sub-committee release

Competition administration
- Record matches played outside the App directly against the competition draw. Submit and confirm the result separately, advancing the winner and updating the printable draw.
- Admins can arrange competition fixtures on behalf of players. Both entries receive booking confirmations and rescheduling updates.
- Add a preliminary match to an eligible published draw when an entry has been missed, with a review step before applying the amendment.

Handicap competitions
- Record individual player handicaps and calculate the team's combined handicap. The lower-handicap team receives the difference as its starting score.
- Individual handicaps and team totals appear in entries, draws, match details, booking details and draw PDFs. Known matchups show their calculated starting scores.
- Next-round emails now include the individual handicaps, combined totals and starting scores for both teams.

Electronic scorecards
- Both parties can review and sign one shared electronic scorecard on the scorer's device. The opponent does not need a separate App login to sign.
- Separate-account agreement remains available. Corrections invalidate signatures and agreements so the revised scores must be acknowledged again.
- Signed scorecards can be viewed and shared as PDFs. Competition Secretary confirmation remains a separate step before a winner advances.

Clearer Dashboard
- Completed competition matches are removed from upcoming competition fixtures and do not reappear under accepted fixtures after refresh.
- Winners' next matches appear once both opponents are confirmed; next-round emails are generated automatically.

Please check the above workflows on your iPhone and report anything confusing or unexpected. This TestFlight build connects to production: competition changes, bookings and emails are real.
